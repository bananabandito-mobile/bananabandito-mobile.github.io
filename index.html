Built for gym owners, instructors, and staff

One app for the people already on your floor: members, announcements, class RSVPs, staff messaging, waivers, and membership billing.

Join with a code, approve in the app

After billing setup, share a join code. Members request access; the owner approves who gets into the community.

Announcements that actually get seen

Post updates to the community bulletin. Members see them in the app, with push notifications when the floor needs to know now.

Schedule, RSVP, and assign instructors

Publish classes and sessions, take RSVPs, target who should see a session, and assign an instructor — without a separate calendar tool.

Staff inbox and class channels

Members message the gym; staff work the inbox and class channels. Messaging stays operational — members do not DM each other.

Waivers and documents, e-signed

Send PDFs for members to review and e-sign in the app, so waivers and policies live with the membership record.

Membership plans, households, and payouts

Create membership plans, bill households, add dependents, and receive payouts through Stripe Connect.

Owner analytics

Review member counts, join requests, RSVP totals, and billing snapshots in the app. Built for a single gym or studio — not an enterprise dashboard.

How Elite Member IQ fits your week

  1. 1. Create your community

    Sign up as Owner, name your gym or studio, and set staff roles (Manager, Instructor, Staff, Billing Admin).

  2. 2. Connect billing before you invite anyone

    Connect Stripe Connect, add membership plans, and turn on household billing so payouts and plans are ready before the first join code goes out.

  3. 3. Share a join code and approve members

    Members enter your code and wait for approval. Add dependents to a household after the member is in.

  4. 4. Publish classes, announcements, and documents

    Build the schedule with RSVPs and instructor assignment, post announcements, and send waivers for e-sign.

  5. 5. Run the week from the app

    Staff use the inbox and class channels; members RSVP and get push updates; owners review activity and billing.

Privacy at a glance

Elite Member IQ is built for small gyms. We collect only what we need to run the app. How Firebase, Stripe, and our hosting providers handle data is in the Privacy Policy.

Firebase-backed

We use Firebase for authentication, community data, file storage, push notifications, App Check, and Crashlytics for reliability.

Payments via Stripe

When billing is enabled, memberships and gym payouts use Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.

No data sales

We do not sell your personal information or use targeted advertising. Data is used to provide and improve Elite Member IQ.

Control in the app

Update your profile, manage notifications, and message your gym in-app. Privacy and deletion requests go to Banana Bandito LLC by email.
