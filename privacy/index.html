Elite Member IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026 · This policy describes how Elite Member IQ handles personal data for users of the app and this website.

Elite Member IQ is a mobile hub for small gyms, dojos, bootcamps, and trainers. We use Firebase, Stripe (when billing is enabled), and platform providers like Apple and Google to run the app, and we collect only the data we need to provide the service.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Elite Member IQ mobile application (the “App”) and this marketing website (together, the “Services”).

“We”, “us”, and “our” refer to Banana Bandito LLC, the company behind Elite Member IQ. The Services are currently focused on users in the United States, starting with Texas, and may expand to additional states and countries over time. When we expand into new regions or add new tools, we will update this policy as needed.

Elite Member IQ includes community membership, announcements, scheduling with RSVPs, staff messaging (Chat), documents and e-sign, and optional Stripe-powered billing (platform fees, member subscriptions, and Connect payouts) when a community enables it. Community owners, managers, instructors, and staff may see member data within their community as needed to operate the gym.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the information we need to run Elite Member IQ and keep your community functioning smoothly.

Account and profile information

  • Account details: your name and email address. Accounts use email and password only. Firebase Authentication stores that password as a hashed credential — not in plain text in our database. We do not offer Sign in with Apple, Google, or other social login providers.
  • Profile (optional): display name and phone number if you choose to provide them. We do not collect date of birth.
  • Role and membership: whether you are an owner, manager, instructor, staff member, billing admin, member, or dependent; which community you belong to; membership status; permission flags; and join-code onboarding.
  • Notification registration: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to deliver push notifications to your device.

Dependents and household

  • Dependent profiles: display name and plan or billing linkage for household members who do not have their own login (including children under 13). We do not collect date of birth, and we do not give under-13 dependents a login, chat access, or a push-notification token.
  • Guardian relationship: the primary member linked to a dependent profile. Staff and the household primary see that profile in the community as needed to run the gym.

Community and content data

  • Community details: gym or community name, address, phone, logo or branding, join code, and basic settings.
  • Bulletin and announcements: posts, messages, and images you upload to share with your members.
  • Schedule and events: classes, camps, sessions, dates and times, RSVP status, session assignments, instructor assignment, and occurrence exceptions (for example canceling or rescheduling a single class).
  • Messaging: conversation metadata; message text; images uploaded to staff inbox, announcements, community channels, or class/cohort chats; and moderation records (reports, hide or delete actions, internal staff-only notes).
  • Documents: uploaded PDFs, titles and descriptions, assignments, and e-sign records (consent text, typed signer name, signed PDF copy, and signing timestamp).

Billing and payment data

  • Billing configuration: membership plans, pricing rules, and household, trial, or registration-fee policy.
  • Payment identifiers and status: Stripe customer, subscription, and Connect account IDs; payment status; billing type; and invoice or snapshot references. Full card or bank numbers are entered in Stripe-hosted flows and are not stored in our database.
  • Platform and member billing: community default payment method confirmation via Stripe, member subscriptions, household plan assignments, and member-chosen amounts where enabled.

Usage and activity data

  • Activity history: records of certain in-app events (such as announcements, chat, documents signed, assigned sessions, and billing alerts) so that you and your members can see a history of activity.
  • Owner analytics: aggregated counts computed in the app from community data (for example member counts, join requests, and RSVP totals). This is not advertising analytics.

Device and technical data

  • Device information: such as device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • Crash and error reports: Firebase Crashlytics crash and non-fatal reports (stack traces and crash metadata). We do not set payment or contact identifiers as Crashlytics custom keys.
  • App Check signals: device and app attestation tokens (for example Play Integrity or App Attest) used to verify genuine app traffic.
  • Technical logs: limited diagnostic and error information, including server-side logs for billing webhooks. We do not log full card data.

3. How we use data

We use the information described above for the following:

To provide and operate the app

  • To create and manage user accounts and communities.
  • To display announcements, schedules, activity history, documents, and conversations to the right people.
  • To send in-app and push notifications for key updates (for example join requests, announcements, chat, and assigned sessions).
  • To assign, display, and e-sign PDF documents, and to store signed copies.
  • To collect platform fees, member subscriptions, and Connect payouts when billing is enabled, including household pricing and payment-failure alerts.
  • To support guardian-managed dependent profiles (no login or chat for children under 13) and member accounts for users 13 and older.

To maintain safety and reliability

  • To monitor for abuse, misuse, or technical issues.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Services, including Firebase App Check on client and backend calls.
  • To diagnose crashes with Firebase Crashlytics.
  • To enforce our terms and comply with applicable law.

To communicate with you

  • To respond to privacy, deletion, or account requests you send to Banana Bandito LLC using the contact details in Section 12.
  • To send important notices about changes to the Services or this policy.

We do not currently use your data for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing and third parties

We share information only when necessary to operate Elite Member IQ, comply with the law, or with your direction.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers to run the Services. These providers process data on our behalf and only as instructed by us. Key providers include:

  • Stripe: payment processing for community platform billing, member subscriptions (including household plans), and Stripe Connect payouts to communities. Stripe receives payment method details directly; we store Stripe identifiers, billing status, and invoice or subscription references in Firebase — not full card or bank account numbers.
  • Firebase (Google): authentication; Firestore database; Cloud Storage (logos, bulletin images, chat images, PDF documents and signed copies); Cloud Functions; Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications); Firebase App Check (app attestation); and Firebase Crashlytics (crash and error reports).
  • Apple and Google: mobile platform providers and (when published) app store distribution. We do not use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. Elite Member IQ does not use Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchase billing for gym memberships; payments are processed through Stripe when billing is enabled.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare: this marketing website is hosted on GitHub Pages (including GitHub’s content delivery network) and served through Cloudflare on our custom domain. They process standard request metadata (such as IP address, user-agent, and requested URL) to deliver the site over HTTPS. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Within your community

Community owners, managers, staff, and instructors (as permitted by role) can view information needed to run the gym — for example member profiles, RSVP and session assignment status, billing status, chat threads they manage, and document assignments. Members see content shared with them (announcements, schedule, assigned documents, and conversations they participate in).

Messaging your gym through Chat routes messages to community staff. That is not a direct line to Banana Bandito LLC unless we operate that community.

Legal and safety

  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • To enforce our terms, protect our rights, or investigate fraud or security issues.

Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, in which case we will take steps to ensure appropriate protections are in place.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use third-party advertising networks. We use Firebase Crashlytics (part of Firebase/Google) for crash reporting only, without setting payment or contact identifiers as custom keys.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This marketing website does not set cookies and does not use browser storage such as localStorage or sessionStorage. We do not use cookies or similar technologies on this website for advertising or analytics.

Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may process standard web request data to serve the site, provide HTTPS, and maintain security. Those providers may set their own cookies as described in their privacy policies.

The mobile app uses Firebase and platform SDKs that may store technical identifiers or tokens on your device to support sign-in, data sync, and notifications. The app also uses Firebase App Check, which may exchange device attestation signals with Google to confirm the app is genuine before allowing access to backend services. You can manage certain settings (such as notifications) through your device’s system settings.

6. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

  • Account and community data are retained while your account and community remain active.
  • Messages and chat images are retained while the conversation or community exists and as needed for moderation.
  • Signed documents are retained for record-keeping until deleted as part of community or account lifecycle.
  • Billing records (Stripe identifiers, snapshots, and payment status) are retained while billing is active and as required for tax or accounting.
  • Crash reports are retained per Firebase Crashlytics default retention.
  • FCM tokens are removed on sign-out. Activity and technical logs may be kept for a shorter period to help us troubleshoot issues and keep the Services secure.

When data is no longer needed, we will delete it or de-identify it where appropriate.

7. Your rights and choices

Because Elite Member IQ is currently focused on users in the United States, starting with Texas, your specific rights may vary by state. Regardless of where you are, we aim to give you meaningful control over your information.

  • Access and updates: you can review and update certain account and profile details directly in the app.
  • Account deletion: you can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us using the contact details in Section 12. We may ask you to verify that the request comes from the account holder.
  • Notifications: you can manage push notifications through your device and app settings.
  • Billing: where Stripe flows are enabled, you can manage payment methods and subscriptions in the app (member self-service and owner billing screens).
  • Dependent controls: primary members manage dependent profiles. Members aged 13–17 who have their own login can update their own profile; gym staff and a linked adult in the household can still see that member in the community.
  • Community exit: where available in the app, you can leave a community from the app. Leaving a community is not the same as deleting your Elite Member IQ account.

8. Children, dependents, and age-related information

Elite Member IQ is designed for gym owners, managers, staff, instructors, and members. You must be at least 13 years old to create an Elite Member IQ account. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not create logins, chat access, or push-notification tokens for children under 13.

A primary member may add dependents under 13 (for example family members or youth athletes) as household profiles. Those profiles include a display name and billing linkage provided by the adult member or gym staff. We do not collect date of birth. Under-13 dependents cannot sign in or use Chat.

Members aged 13–17 may have their own email-and-password account. That account is supervised in the gym: community staff and a linked adult in the household can see and manage that member’s community record (for example roster, RSVPs, documents, and billing) as they would for other members.

Communities may offer youth-oriented membership plans. Billing for dependents and teens may be included in household subscriptions managed by the primary member.

If you believe we have collected a child’s personal information without appropriate guardian involvement, contact us using the details in Section 12.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including the security features provided by Firebase, Stripe, GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare.

  • Encryption in transit for data between the app and backend.
  • Restricted access to production systems.
  • Firebase App Check on the client and on HTTPS callables to help verify genuine app traffic.
  • Stripe handles PCI-scoped payment data; we store Stripe identifiers and billing status, not full card or bank numbers.
  • Chat and document storage paths are scoped by community and authentication rules.
  • Monitoring for unusual or abusive activity.

However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. We work to protect your data but cannot promise absolute security.

10. International data transfers

Even though we are currently focused on users in the United States, some of our service providers (such as Firebase/Google and Stripe) may process or store data in data centers located in other regions.

Where data is transferred or stored outside your state or country, we rely on the safeguards provided by those infrastructure providers and the contractual arrangements we have in place with them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our Services, data practices, or legal requirements change.

  • We will update the “Last updated” date at the top.
  • For material changes, we may also provide additional notice in the app.

If you continue to use Elite Member IQ after a change takes effect, you agree to the updated policy.

12. How to contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, or to request access, correction, or deletion:

  • Email elitememberiq-support@bananabandito.com with your account email, community name, and request type (access, correction, deletion, or other privacy question).
  • For billing or account issues within a community, you may also use Chat in the app to message your gym. That routes messages to community staff, not to Banana Bandito LLC, unless we operate that community.

As Elite Member IQ expands to new regions or adds more tools, we may provide additional region-specific contact details in the future.
